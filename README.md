# Chromacoustic RDJ

**Free.** See Rekordbox's waveforms, browser and FX on your Android tablet, over Wi-Fi or USB tethering.

## Get it

1. **Windows (PC next to Rekordbox):** [Chromacoustic RDJ on the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P4L0SL9X5Z4)
2. **Android tablet:** download **Chromacoustic_RDJ_Tablet_0.3.16.apk** from [Releases](../../releases/latest), open it on the tablet and tap Install
   (allow "Install unknown apps" for your file manager if Android asks).

## Use it

1. Start RDJ on the PC and open Rekordbox 7.
2. Open RDJ on the tablet - it finds the PC by itself. Approve the tablet once on the PC.
3. On the PC: press REFRESH, pick WAVEFORM, FX and TRACKLIST and drag a box round each on the preview, then SAVE.
   RESET clears the boxes so you can draw them again.

Rekordbox can be behind other windows, but not minimised.
For gigs, USB tethering from the tablet is the most reliable connection.

## Needs

- Windows 10 or 11, Rekordbox 7
- Android 8 or newer tablet

## Privacy

No account, no adverts, no tracking - RDJ only talks to your own tablet. [Privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md)

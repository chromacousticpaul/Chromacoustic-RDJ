# Chromacoustic RDJ - Privacy policy

Chromacoustic RDJ is free software made by a single developer in the UK.

**What RDJ does with your data:** nothing leaves your own network. RDJ runs on your PC and
talks only to the Chromacoustic RDJ app on your own tablet, over your Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

- No account, no sign-in.
- No adverts, analytics or tracking.
- RDJ does not send anything to the internet.
- What it keeps on your PC: the list of tablets you approved, your layout settings and a
  local log file. Uninstalling RDJ removes them.
- It captures the Rekordbox window and reads your Rekordbox XML export only to show them on your tablet.

Questions: open an issue on the Chromacoustic RDJ GitHub page.
